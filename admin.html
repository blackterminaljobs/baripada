How Staff Payment Works

Current payment calculation rules

Payment calculation:

Total Payable = Base Salary + (Unique Visited Students × Visit Incentive) + (Unique Converted Students × Admission Incentive)

Raw inquiries do not generate incentive.
Duplicate mobile numbers are not counted twice for the same incentive category.
Payment is calculated for the current month.
Coordinator settings and Telecaller settings are stored separately.
